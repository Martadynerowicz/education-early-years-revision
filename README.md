# Education and Early Years Revision

An **independent revision resource** for the NCFE CACHE T Level in Education and
Early Years. It is **not endorsed by NCFE, CACHE or the DfE**.

- Live site: https://martadynerowicz.github.io/education-early-years-revision/
- Privacy notice: https://martadynerowicz.github.io/education-early-years-revision/privacy.html
- AI notice: https://martadynerowicz.github.io/education-early-years-revision/ai-notice.html

This is the **college version**. The app has no AI of its own and sends nothing
to any AI service. Written questions have a **Check with AI (Copilot)** button:
after an on-device safeguarding / personal-data check, it copies the question,
mark-scheme points and the learner's answer to the clipboard and opens
Microsoft Copilot (copilot.microsoft.com) in a new tab. The learner chooses to
paste it there, signed in with their college Microsoft account. The button is
hidden in research mode. Nothing is recorded or stored.

Everything else you do stays in your own browser (local storage). Nothing is
uploaded. Use "Delete my data" on the research page to clear it.
