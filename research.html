Research mode

Research mode is for a class project run by your teacher (a PGCE student at the University of Staffordshire). Only use it if your teacher asked you, and you agreed on the paper form.

  • Taking part is your choice. It does not affect your marks or grades.
  • You use a code, not your name.
  • Everything is saved only on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • At the end, you can choose to hand in a results file. You can say no.
  • You can stop at any time and delete your data. You do not need to give a reason.
  • While research mode is on, AI features are off and your written answers are not saved.
