AI notice

Microsoft Copilot is AI — not your teacher or an NCFE examiner. It can make mistakes.

What the AI option is

  • Check with AI (Copilot): after you write your own answer to a written question, the button copies the question, the mark-scheme points and your answer. It opens Microsoft Copilot in a new tab. You sign in with your college account, paste (Ctrl+V) and press send. Copilot then lists the mark-scheme points you covered and missed, with a hint for each missed point.
  • This app does not have its own AI on this website. The app's "Ask AI" and "Check with AI" features, which use a different AI service, are switched off here.

What they are not

  • The AI is not a teacher and not an NCFE examiner.
  • It does not give official marks or grades. Its feedback is practice only.
  • It can be wrong, out of date or miss things. Check important points with your teacher, your notes or the official specification.
  • Do not use it for coursework or anything that is assessed. That could count as malpractice.

How to use it well

  1. Try first. Write your own answer or ideas before you ask.
  2. Use the hints to improve your answer yourself.
  3. Compare with the model answer and mark scheme in the app.
  4. Ask your teacher or a classmate about anything you are unsure of.

Your data

  • This app sends nothing to any AI service. The button only copies text to your clipboard and opens Copilot.
  • Nothing is sent unless you paste it into Copilot and press send. If you do, Microsoft processes it under your college account, as covered by your college's agreement with Microsoft. Your college's IT and acceptable-use rules apply.
  • Do not write names or personal details about you, children or staff, and do not add them in Copilot.
  • The app checks your answer first. It will not copy text that looks like it has an email address, phone number, postcode, name or a worrying message.
  • This app does not store or record what you copy, or whether you used Copilot.
  • More detail is in the privacy notice.

When the AI option is switched off

  • In research mode.
  • In the Android app.

If something is worrying you

The AI cannot help with personal problems and nobody reads your messages. Talk to a trusted adult, your tutor or the college safeguarding team. Childline: 0800 1111 (free, 24 hours). Shout: text SHOUT to 85258. In an emergency, call 999.
