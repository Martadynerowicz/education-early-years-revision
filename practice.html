Command words

Identify · 1–2 marks
Name, list or otherwise characterise. No development is required.
State · 1–2 marks
Express something clearly and briefly in definite terms.
Outline · 2–4 marks
Set out the main characteristics or points without full detail.
Describe · 2–4 marks
Give an account of the main features. More detail than outline, but little or no reasoning.
Explain · 2–6 marks
Set out purposes, reasons or mechanisms. Say how or why, often with a linked example.
Discuss · 6–15 marks
Present key points about different ideas or arguments and draw them together.
Analyse · 6–12 marks
Break information into parts, examine relationships, and say what the consequences or implications are.
Evaluate · 9–15 marks
Make a reasoned judgement from available evidence, weighing strengths, limitations and alternatives.
Justify · 6–15 marks
Present a reasoned case for a choice, supported by evidence, theory or statutory requirements.
Compare · 4–8 marks
Identify similarities and/or differences between two or more things.

How the 2025 core is assessed

  • Core exam — One core examination, 2 hours 30 minutes, 140 marks (128 plus 12 QWC). Section A: Element 2 Supporting education and Element 6 Working in partnership (36 marks). Section B: Element 3 Safeguarding, equality and diversity and wellbeing (31). Section C: Element 4 SEND (28). Section D: Element 5 Child development (33). Elements 1 and 7 are primarily ESP but can appear in the exam. Apply named legislation and theory to UK settings.
  • AO1 — Recall knowledge
  • AO2 — Apply knowledge to contexts
  • AO3 — Analyse and evaluate
  • ESP — The employer-set project is 15 hours 45 minutes, 90 marks, and is 40% of the core. It assesses Element 1 Wider education sector and Element 7 Professional practice, plus core skills CS1–CS4. Tasks typically include an early support or intervention plan, an activity plan, reflection on peer feedback, a presentation, and a reflective account. This practice paper compresses the thinking into three extended tasks you can attempt untimed.
  • OS — Early Years Educator synoptic assignments (21–26 hours). Assignment 1 covers placement observations (including EYE competence criteria). Assignment 2 is planning educational activities. Assignment 3 includes observation, reflection and scenario-based questions (Part 2 is typically 2 hours). All five performance outcomes are assessed.
